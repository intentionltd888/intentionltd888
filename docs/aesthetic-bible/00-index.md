# Aloha jie 美學聖經 · Aesthetic Bible

這份文件把 Aloha jie 的 AI 意象短片、Pinterest 圖板、生成做法和剪輯習慣，徹底消化成一套有文化脈絡、可以照著做的視覺 IP 準則，給人看，也給 INTENTION OS（`aloha-aesthetic` skill）用。

<details><summary>English</summary>

A digested, culturally grounded visual-IP guide distilled from Aloha jie's AI short films, Pinterest boards, generation methods and editing habits. Written for both humans and the `aloha-aesthetic` skill.
</details>
