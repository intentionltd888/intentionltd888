## Intention Made

我們從 4 年前就開始在探索生成式 AI 的應用，團隊內的導演在還沒有出現 AI 生成影片時就在探索生成圖片可以怎麼應用，後來發現永遠也追不完，科技發達的程度讓我們處在擔憂與期待中來回折騰。

從追逐改成找到平衡，於是便多開啟了支線：INTENTION made。

我們會在這個帳號及官方網站上，不定期地發佈我們創作時使用的工具，一部分想直接跟市場做驗證，一部分想保持「創造」的初衷：

1. 一開始對於成品的某種幻想
2. 進入修羅場的打磨、驗證過程
3. 再修一輪，考慮使用者的體驗
4. 添加上我們對於視覺美感的期待

其中不乏會遇到很多技術瓶頸、不切實際的幻想，但「探索創造過程的樂趣」實在是太爽了。

會不定期更新或是品質有限，是因為這完全是非我們本業的額外大型、巨型 Project，消耗掉的不只是架設、建置的成本，還有團隊大家本該拿去休息的時間。

總而言之，希望這些工具幫得上你，一起享受創造這件事情！Yes sir ski.

<summary>English</summary>

We started exploring generative AI four years ago. Our director was already testing what generated images could do before AI video existed. Then we realised we would never catch up: the pace of the technology kept us bouncing between worry and anticipation.

So we stopped chasing and looked for balance instead. That is how the side track began: INTENTION made.

On this account and on our website we will publish, from time to time, the tools we use while we create. Partly to test them against the market, partly to keep the original point of "making things":

1. The first fantasy of what the finished thing could be
2. The grind of polishing and testing it
3. Another pass, this time thinking about the person using it
4. Adding what we expect of it visually

Along the way there are technical walls and unrealistic fantasies. But the fun of exploring the making itself is too good to give up.

Updates are irregular and quality has its limits, because none of this is our day job. It is a large, extra project that costs not only setup and build time, but the hours the team should have spent resting.

In short: we hope these tools are useful to you. Enjoy making things with us. Yes sir ski.
